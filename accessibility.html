Accessibility Statement

Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016 · WCAG 2.1 Level AA

The Odisha Janta Congress Party believes that every Odia — including the approximately 12 lakh persons with disabilities in Odisha — deserves equal access to information about the party, our manifesto, and our public commitments. This commitment flows from our manifesto's Viklanga Samartha chapter (Chapter 11), which commits OJC to genuine inclusion as a constitutional right.

1. Our Commitment

OJC is working toward conformance with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA and the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016. We treat accessibility as a continuing obligation, not a one-time project.

2. Features Currently in Place

  • Semantic HTML with proper heading hierarchy (one H1 per page, logical H2/H3 nesting)
  • Alternative text for content images
  • Sufficient colour contrast between text and background (WCAG 2.1 AA)
  • Resizable text up to 200% without loss of content or functionality
  • Full keyboard navigation support across menus, forms, and interactive elements
  • Skip-to-content link at the top of each page
  • Form labels explicitly associated with every input
  • Required field indicators that do not rely on colour alone
  • Mobile-responsive design for phones, tablets, and small screens
  • Bilingual content (Odia and English) with lang attributes
  • ARIA live regions for form confirmation messages

4. Requesting Alternative Formats

If you need information in large print, audio, Braille, plain text, Easy-Read, or Odia voice format — we will provide it free of charge.

Contact: accessibility@ojcp.org.in · +91 9777770323

We respond within 5 working days and provide the requested format within 15 working days.

5. Reporting Barriers

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, please tell us: (1) what you were trying to do, (2) what went wrong, and (3) what assistive technology you were using, if any.

Send to: accessibility@ojcp.org.in

We acknowledge within 24 hours and fix barriers or provide workarounds within 30 days for most issues.

6. Event Accessibility

For all OJC events including the April 8 founding launch: wheelchair access provided where venue permits; reserved seating for senior citizens and PwD; sign-language interpretation on request (48 hours notice); accessible toilets confirmed in advance.

To request event accommodations: accessibility@ojcp.org.in or call +91 9777770323 — at least 48 hours before the event.

7. Governing Law

This Statement is provided under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016 and the Information Technology Act 2000, governed by the laws of India, subject to the jurisdiction of courts at Bhubaneswar, Odisha.
