Vision Statistics — All Source-Cited

For press use with attribution to cited source

HEALTHCARE
  • – 60% of sanctioned doctor posts vacant — source: State Health Minister to the Assembly (2024–25).
  • – SCB Medical College — 10 deaths
  • – 453 maternal deaths per 1 lakh births – national average: 88 (SRS 2021–23)
  • – 1,735 doctor-patient ratio vs WHO standard of 1:1000
  • – 46.4% children aged 0–59 months anaemic (NFHS-5)
  • – 64.4% women aged 15–49 anaemic (NFHS-5)
EDUCATION
  • – Secondary school dropout rate — 15%, against a national average of 11.5% — source: NITI Aayog.
  • – 40% of Class III rural students cannot read a Class I text (ASER 2024)
  • – 70% of Class II students cannot do basic subtraction
  • – 68% of state university faculty posts vacant
  • – 150–5.47 lakh children left school during 2018–23 (CAG)
MINERALS & ECONOMY
  • – 43.7% of India's mineral wealth — source: OES 2025–26.
  • – Per capita income — ₹1.86 lakh, 16% below the national average — source: MoSPI 2025–26.
  • – 1,237 villages — source: IT Minister to the Assembly, March 2026
  • – Iron Ore Rank 1 (55%) – Chromite Rank 1 (100%) – Bauxite Rank 1 (72.7%)
  • – ~80% of state non-tax revenue from minerals
WOMEN, TRIBAL & WATER
  • – Women's LFPR — 48.7%, significantly higher than the national average of 40.3% — source: PLFS 2023–24.
  • – 40,947 crimes against women between July 2024 and September 2025 — source: data provided by CM Mohan Majhi in the Assembly on 8 December 2025.
  • – Conviction rate — 6.9% (2023, 3-year low) – ~90.8% cases pending
  • – 62 Scheduled Tribes – ~93 lakh people – 22% of Odisha
  • – ~71 lakh SC Odias (17.1%) – 39.3% OBC (2023 Caste Survey)
  • – ~50 lakh farmers depend on Mahanadi – Tribunal referred 2018
