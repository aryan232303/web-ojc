Sakhyama Odisha Manifesto 2026 Released – 15 Unbreakable Pledges, Every Commitment With a Deadline

The complete Odisha Janta Congress Party Manifesto is now publicly available – 12 chapters, 15 Unbreakable Pledges, and a Vision 2036 for Odisha. Every promise carries a timeline, a defined mechanism, and a standard of public accountability.

April 27, 2026 · OJC Official Statement · Read the Manifesto →
Policy

OJC Pledges Odia-First Jobs – 50% Reservation in Skilled Private-Sector Employment

Pledge 5 of the OJC Manifesto establishes an Odia-first framework for private and public employment, committing 50% reservation for Odia youth in skilled private-sector jobs and 100% in unskilled positions.

April 25, 2026
Policy

Statutory Mahanadi Water Rights Authority Will Be Established in Year One of OJC Government

OJC reaffirms Pledge 14 commitment to establish a statutory Mahanadi Water Rights Authority within the first year of forming government. 50 lakh farmers depend on Mahanadi. Tribunal referred in 2018. Odisha cannot wait further.

April 22, 2026
Policy

Senior Citizen Pension Raised Five-Fold – ₹500 to ₹2,500 Per Month Under Barishtanka Samman Yojana

Chapter 9 of the Sakhyama Odisha Manifesto commits to a five-fold pension increase for senior citizens, alongside free health check-ups, free medicines for chronic conditions, and free travel on state buses.

April 18, 2026
Event

OJC Formally Launches at Exhibition Ground – 15 Pledges Read Aloud Before Supporters from All 30 Districts

The Odisha Janta Congress Party was formally constituted at a founding launch event at Exhibition Ground, Bhubaneswar, with representatives from all 30 districts. The Sakhyama Odisha Manifesto 2026 was released.

April 8, 2026
Policy

Cashless ₹10 Lakh Healthcare Coverage for Every Odia Family – Pledge 11 Detail Released

The Odisha Universal Health Scheme under Pledge 11 – Palli Chikitsa – will provide cashless healthcare up to ₹10 lakh per family per year at government and empanelled private hospitals, with one medical college per district within 10 years.

April 12, 2026
Policy

Mahalaxmi Scheme: ?12,000 Per Year Directly to 30 Lakh BPL Women From Year One

Pledge 8 – Nari Sasaktikarana – commits ₹12,000 annually to the senior-most woman in every BPL household via direct bank transfer, covering an estimated 30 lakh families from the first year of OJC forming government.

April 15, 2026

Manifesto Statistics – All Source-Cited For press use with attribution to cited source

Healthcare

  • 153 maternal deaths per 1 lakh births – national average: 88 (SRS 2021–23)
  • 1:1,735 doctor-patient ratio vs WHO standard of 1:1,000
  • 37% of sanctioned doctor posts vacant in state hospitals
  • 64.2% children aged 6–59 months anaemic (NFHS-5)
  • 64.3% women aged 15–49 anaemic (NFHS-5)
  • SCB Medical College fire, March 2026 – 12 lives lost
OJC Policy: ₹10L cashless health - 30 district medical colleges - Aarogya Ratha vans - Pledge 11

Education

  • 17.7% secondary dropout rate (2022–23) – above national 16.4%
  • 40% of Class III rural students can read a Class II text (ASER 2024)
  • 70% of Class III students cannot do basic subtraction
  • 68% of state university faculty posts vacant
  • 1.50–5.47 lakh children left school during 2018-23 (CAG)
OJC Policy: Sarba Shiksha Utkarsha – Aasa School Jibaa – ₹10L Education Loan Guarantee - Pledges 1 & 2

Minerals & Economy

  • 43.7% of India's mineral production value from Odisha (IBM 2022–23)
  • Iron Ore Rank 1 (55%) – Chromite Rank 1 (100%) – Bauxite Rank 1 (72.7%)
  • 80% of state non-tax revenue from minerals
  • Per capita income: ?1.65L - 22% below national ₹2.11L (MoSPI 2023–24)
  • 1,237 villages without mobile connectivity (March 2026)
OJC Policy: 30% royalty ring-fenced - 20% community equity - 50%/100% Odia jobs reservation - Pledge 15 & 5

Women, Tribal & Water

  • Women LFPR: 48.7% - among lowest in India
  • 40,947 crimes against women Jul 2024–Sep 2025
  • Conviction rate: 6.9% (2023, 5-year low) – 90.8% cases pending
  • 62 Scheduled Tribes – 93 lakh people – 22% of Odisha
  • 71 lakh SC Odias (17.1%) - 39.3% OBC (2023 Caste Survey)
  • 50 lakh farmers depend on Mahanadi – Tribunal referred 2018
OJC Policy: Mahalaxmi ?12,000/yr - 50% women candidates - FRA in 12 months - Water Authority year one
