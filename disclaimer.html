1. Informational Accuracy

OJC takes care to publish accurate information. Statistics cited from sources such as the Indian Bureau of Mines (IBM), Sample Registration System (SRS), National Family Health Survey (NFHS), Annual Status of Education Report (ASER), CAG, and MoSPI are correct as of the most recent edition referenced. These figures may be revised by their issuing bodies.

2. Vision & Forward-Looking Statements

The Sakhyama Odisha Vision 2026 sets out OJC's policy direction and 16 Unbreakable Pledges. These are public goals made in good faith. Their realisation is subject to:

  • OJC forming the Government of Odisha through a democratic mandate
  • Approval and passage of necessary legislation
  • Allocation of state and central budgetary resources
  • Decisions of competent courts on legal challenges
  • Reasonable operational and force-majeure conditions

OJC will publish progress against every pledge annually through a State of Odisha Report Card and a real-time public Governance Dashboard.
