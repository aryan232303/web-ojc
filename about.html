Chapter 1 & 2 · Party Profile

Who We Are & Why We Exist

The Odisha Janta Congress Party — a regional movement of farmers, women, youth, tribal communities, professionals, and citizens who share one conviction: Odisha belongs to Odias. Founded 2026.

Shri Mohammed Moquim

Founding President · OJC

Message from the Founding President

Jai Jagannath! Mora Priya Odishabasimaane,

The people of Odisha have endured decades of broken promises, weak accountability, and governance that too often serves the powerful instead of the common citizen. Farmers struggle. Youth migrate to other states for work. Small businesses fight to survive. Basic public services fail to meet expectations.

This is not because Odisha lacks resources or talent. It is because governance has lacked honesty, direction, and the will to put people first. The Odisha Janta Congress Party has a clear conviction: Odisha belongs to Odias. Its rivers, land, forests, economy, and institutions must work for the people of this state, not for a privileged few.

"In a nation of 140 crore, even if we need to make just one needle, we have the strength of 140 crore hands. Together we can create not one, but 140 crore needles. This is the power of collective effort. Odisha must harness this strength to build industries, generate employment, and create opportunities driven by its own people."

The priorities are simple and urgent. Create jobs by supporting local industries and ensuring Odias are hired first. Strengthen agriculture by guaranteeing fair prices, irrigation, and direct support to farmers. Improve education and healthcare so that every family — village or city — has access to quality services.

This manifesto commits to clean governance. Every policy and scheme will be backed by verifiable data, clear budgeting, and public accountability. The era of empty announcements must end and be replaced by measurable performance.

The Odisha Janta Congress Party stands for a government that listens, acts, and delivers. The goal is clear — a stronger, fairer, and self-reliant Odisha for all 4.6 crore people, within a decade.

Shri Mohammed Moquim
Founding President, Odisha Janta Congress Party · 2026

Chapter 2 · Core Ideology

Seven pillars. One conviction.
Odisha for Odias.

The Odisha Janta Congress Party brings together citizens who share the conviction that Odisha has been underserved by governments focused on agendas other than the priorities of this state.

PILLAR 01

People-Led Development

Development driven by the needs and participation of citizens, where policies are shaped by people's voices and benefits reach every household — farmer, worker, student, mother, elder.

PILLAR 02

Pro-Farmer

Fair pricing, water security, income stability, and dignity for farmers. Legal MSP guarantee, payment in 7 days, and ₹15,000/year direct income support.

PILLAR 03

Tribal Rights

Uncompromising protection of tribal land, forest rights, cultural identity, and constitutional safeguards. 62 Scheduled Tribes — 93 lakh people, 22% of Odisha — must live with full dignity.

PILLAR 04

Odia Identity

Strong pride in Odia language, culture, and heritage, alongside a firm commitment to safeguarding Odisha's rights, resources, and voice within the Indian Union. Our identity is non-negotiable.

PILLAR 05

Grassroots Democracy

Strengthening Panchayati Raj, empowering local governance, and ensuring decision-making power reaches villages and Gram Sabhas — not just state and national capitals.

PILLAR 06

Social Justice

An equitable society through fair access to opportunities, resources, and public services. SC, ST, OBC, religious minorities, and persons with disabilities receive dedicated, measurable commitments.

PILLAR 07

Secular Politics

Governance based on the Constitution of India, free from religious discrimination. Equal rights, dignity, and protection for every citizen of every faith — Hindu, Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain.

Formation Purpose

Why This Party Was Formed

OJC is a new political movement formed to organise citizens around Odisha-first identity, clean governance, public accountability, and district-level democratic participation.

01

Odisha-first public voice

The party was formed around the belief that Odisha's resources, institutions, and public priorities must answer first to the people of Odisha.

02

Clean governance standard

OJC stands for politics where public promises are written, budgeted, measured, and opened to citizen scrutiny.

03

Citizen organisation

The movement is being built through membership, district outreach, local coordination, and direct conversations with supporters across Odisha.

04

Accountability before power

As a newly formed party, OJC seeks public trust through a clear manifesto, transparent organisation, and future intent that citizens can question.
