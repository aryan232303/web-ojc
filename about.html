Who We Are &
Why We Exist

The Odisha Janta Congress Party — a regional movement of farmers, women, youth, tribal communities, professionals, and citizens who share one conviction: Odisha belongs to Odias.

Shri Mohammed Moquim

Founding President · OJC

Message from the Founding President

Bande Utkal Janani!
Mora Priya Odishabasimaane,

The people of Odisha have endured decades of broken expectations, weak accountability, and governance that too often serves the powerful instead of the common citizen. Farmers struggle. Youth migrate to other states for work. Small businesses fight to survive. Basic public services fail to meet expectations.

This is not because Odisha lacks resources or talent. It is because governance has lacked honesty, direction, and the will to put people first. The Odisha Janta Congress Party has a clear conviction: Odisha belongs to Odias. Its rivers, land, forests, economy, and institutions must work for the people of this state, not for a privileged few.

"In a nation of 140 crore, even if we need to make just one needle, we have the strength of 140 crore hands. Together we can create not one, but 140 crore needles. This is the power of collective effort. Odisha must harness this strength to build industries, generate employment, and create opportunities driven by its own people."

The priorities are simple and urgent. Create jobs by supporting local industries and ensuring Odias are hired first. Strengthen agriculture by guaranteeing fair prices, irrigation, and direct support to farmers. Improve education and healthcare so that every family — village or city — has access to quality services.

This vision commits to clean governance. Every policy and scheme will be backed by verifiable data, clear budgeting, and public accountability. The era of empty announcements must end and be replaced by measurable performance.

The Odisha Janta Congress Party stands for a government that listens, acts, and delivers. The goal is clear — a stronger, fairer, and self-reliant Odisha for all 4.6 crore people, within a decade.

Shri Mohammed Moquim
Founding President, Odisha Janta Congress Party

OUR FLAG · OUR IDENTITY

Three colours. One vision.

01

GOLDEN YELLOW BAND

The prosperity, hope, and enduring strength of our agricultural heritage. The harvest that feeds Odisha. The future we are building.

02

WHITE BAND

Peace, integrity, and transparent governance. The honest foundation on which a self-reliant Odisha will be built.

03

ROYAL BLUE BAND

Odisha's rivers, its coastline, the untapped potential of its people and our commitment to inclusive and equitable development. And, the Mahanadi, which is ours to protect.

CORE IDEOLOGY

Nine pillars. One conviction. Odisha for Odias.

01

Secular Politics

Governance firmly rooted in the Constitution of India, ensuring equality before law and freedom from religious discrimination, division, or majoritarian politics.

02

Women's Empowerment

Commitment to full political, economic, and social equality for women, with guaranteed representation, safety, financial independence, and institutional support.

03

Youth-First

Prioritising education, skill development, employment, and entrepreneurship to ensure that Odisha's youth can build their future within the state.

04

People-Led Development

Development driven by the needs and participation of citizens, where policies are shaped by people's voices and benefits reach every household.

05

Pro-Farmer

Ensuring fair pricing, water security, income stability, and dignity for farmers, recognising them as the backbone of Odisha's economy.

06

Tribal Rights

Uncompromising protection of tribal land, forest rights, cultural identity, and constitutional safeguards, with development that respects and empowers communities.

07

Odia Identity

Strong pride in Odia language, culture, and heritage, alongside a firm commitment to safeguarding Odisha's rights, resources, and voice within the Indian Union.

08

Grassroots Democracy

Strengthening Panchayati Raj institutions, empowering local governance, and ensuring decision-making power reaches villages and communities.

09

Social Justice

Building an equitable society through fair access to opportunities, resources, and public services, ensuring that no section of society is left behind.

FORMATION PURPOSE

Why This Party Was Formed

OJC is a new political movement formed to organise citizens around Odisha-first identity, clean governance, public accountability, and district-level democratic participation.

Odisha at the centre

The party was formed around the belief that Odisha's resources and institutions must answer first to the people of Odisha.

Clean governance standard

OJC stands for politics where public plans are written, budgeted, measured, and opened to citizen scrutiny.

Built by Citizens

The movement is being built through membership, district outreach, local coordination, and direct conversations with supporters across Odisha.

Accountability before power

As a newly formed party, OJC seeks public trust through a clear vision, transparent organisation, and future intent that citizens can question.
