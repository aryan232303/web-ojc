Chapter 5 · Sakhyama Odisha

By 2036, Odisha will be a state where these eight things are true.

Our statehood centenary. Three implementation phases. Eight measurable outcomes.

Education

Every child learns, zero dropouts

Sarba Shiksha Utkarsha · Permanent teacher every classroom · Faculty vacancies filled 3 years · Dropout below 3% · ₹10L Education Loan Guarantee SC/ST/OBC/EWS

Jobs

Every youth finds opportunity within Odisha

25 lakh trained · 10 lakh new private jobs · ₹10,000/month apprenticeship · Industrial corridor operational · 4 IT parks

Women

Women lead with equal power

50% women candidates · LFPR 48.7% → 80% by 2036 · Women's Reservation Act · 33% state commission appointments · 10-min emergency response

Health

Every family has quality healthcare

₹10L cashless per family · 30 medical colleges · Doctor ratio 1:1,735 → 1:1,000 · Aarogya Ratha mobile vans · Nutrition Mission

Farmers

Farmers earn stable dignified incomes

Legal MSP all crops · 7-day payment · ₹15,000/year · ₹3,000 crore cold storage · Mahanadi Water Authority year one

Tribal Rights

Tribal communities live with full rights

All FRA claims 12 months · Tribal Land Protection Act · 100 Eklavya Schools · Tribal Academies 4 districts

Resources

Natural resources create prosperity here

30% royalties ring-fenced · 20% community equity · Downstream Manufacturing Mission · SEZ 4 cities · Industrial corridor

Governance

Governance is transparent, citizen-driven

Public Governance Dashboard · Annual State of Odisha Report Card · Nyaya Saakhi legal aid every block · Odisha One digital platform

Phase 1 · 1–2 Years

Immediate Action

Fill vacancies, launch welfare guarantees, restore governance systems, deliver urgent reforms.

Phase 2 · 3–5 Years

Structural Reform

Strengthen healthcare, education, agriculture, and infrastructure systems across the state.

Phase 3 · 5–10 Years

Sustained Growth

Large-scale job creation, industrial expansion, measurable improvements in human development.

"Every commitment in this manifesto is not just a promise, but a responsibility. It carries a clear timeline, a defined mechanism, and a standard of accountability. We invite the people of Odisha not just to support us, but to hold us accountable — because this manifesto belongs to you."

— Manifesto Closing · OJC 2026

Chapter 4 · Paribartana Paain 15 Pratigyan

All 15 Unbreakable Pledges

Each carries a named scheme, a specific number, a defined deadline, and a public accountability mechanism. Each will be reported annually.

01
ସର୍ବ ଶିକ୍ଷା ଉତ୍କର୍ଷ
Sarba Shiksha Utkarsha
Excellence in Education for All

One qualified, permanent teacher in every classroom. Universal foundational literacy and numeracy by Class III. Secondary school dropout rate reduced to below 3%. Faculty vacancies in state universities filled within 3 years through transparent, merit-based recruitment. PhD Fellowship and Education Loan Guarantee up to ₹10 lakh for SC/ST/OBC/EWS students.

17.7% dropout → below 3%68% faculty vacancies filled in 3 yrs₹10L Education Loan Guarantee
02
ଆଶା ସ୍କୁଲ ଯିବା
Aasa School Jibaa
Come, Let's Go to School

Every out-of-school child brought back into education within 2 years, with targeted interventions for rural, tribal, and vulnerable communities. Mid-day meal expanded to pre-primary. Free smartphones to all government school students from Class VIII onwards.

2-year targetSmartphones from Class VIII64.2% childhood anaemia targeted
03
ଡିଜିଟାଲ ଓଡ଼ିଶା
Digital Odisha
Digital Odisha for Every Citizen

100% broadband connectivity to all Gram Panchayats within 3 years. Free smartphones to all government school students from Class VIII onwards. Digital Sahay Kendras in every panchayat. Odisha One single-window digital platform. Land records fully digitised.

100% GP broadband in 3 years1,237 villages without connectivityOdisha One platform
04
କୌଶଳ ପ୍ରଗତି ଯୋଜନା
Kausala Pragati Yojana
Skill Progress Plan

25 lakh youth trained in industry-relevant skills with 70% placement rate. Kausala Vikas Kendras in every block. Graduate unemployment reduced to below 3%. ₹2,000 crore Startup Odisha Fund. Paid apprenticeship at ₹10,000/month for diploma and degree holders under 25.

25 lakh trainedGraduate unemployment below 3%₹10,000/month apprenticeship₹2,000 crore Startup Fund
05
ରୋଜଗାର ଓଡ଼ିଶା
Rojagar Odisha
Employment Odisha — Odia First

All sanctioned government vacancies filled within 2 years through transparent, merit-based recruitment. Annual Odisha Recruitment Calendar published every January. Odia-first approach in public and private employment: 50% reservation for Odia youth in skilled private-sector jobs and 100% in unskilled jobs. Industrial corridor: Rourkela–Sambalpur–Paradeep–Dhamra. IT Parks in 4 cities.

50% Odia skilled / 100% unskilledAll vacancies filled 2 years10 lakh private jobs by 2036Annual Recruitment Calendar
06
ଯୁବ ଶକ୍ତି
Yuva Shakti & Democratic Reform
Youth Power

50% of OJC's election candidates will be aged 40 or below. Student union elections restored within the first year of OJC forming government. Youth leadership at district level.

50% candidates aged ≤40Student unions restored year one
07
ନିଶା ମୁକ୍ତ ଓଡ଼ିଶା
Nisa Mukta Odisha
Drug-Free, Alcohol-Free Odisha

OJC commits to a phased, enforceable transition to a completely alcohol-free Odisha by 2036. De-addiction and rehabilitation centres in every district. Gram Sabhas empowered to ban alcohol availability locally. Strict enforcement against illicit liquor distribution.

21 lakh affected by alcohol4.9 lakh affected by cannabisFull prohibition by 2036
08
ନାରୀ ସଶକ୍ତିକରଣ
Nari Sasaktikarana
Women's Empowerment

Mahalaxmi Direct Support Scheme: ₹12,000 annually to the senior-most woman in every BPL household. Collateral-free SHG credit up to ₹5 lakh at 2% per annum. Equal Pay for Equal Work enforcement. One-third state government jobs reserved for women from 2026.

₹12,000/year Mahalaxmi30 lakh families year one₹5L SHG credit at 2%1/3 govt jobs for women
09
ଶାସନରେ ନାରୀ ସ୍ୱର
Sasanare Naari ra Swara
Women's Voice in Governance

50% of OJC's election candidates will be women. Women's Reservation Act implemented in full for Odisha. 33% of appointments to state commissions and boards reserved for women. Annual Gender Governance Report Card published.

50% women candidates33% state commission appointmentsAnnual Gender Report Card
10
ନିର୍ଭୟ ନାରୀ
Nirbhaya Naari
Fearless Woman

Mahila Suraksha Commands in every district staffed exclusively by trained women police officers, with a 10-minute emergency response time. Fast-track courts in every district completing trials within 180 days. Helpline 1091 strengthened. CCTV on public transport.

10-minute response time180-day fast-track trials40,947 crimes Jul 2024–Sep 2025
11
ପଲ୍ଲୀ ଚିକିତ୍ସା
Palli Chikitsa
Healthcare Closer to Communities

Odisha Universal Health Scheme — cashless treatment up to ₹10 lakh per family per year at all government and empanelled private hospitals. Government medical college in each of 30 districts within 10 years. Aarogya Ratha mobile health vans for tribal and rural areas. Odisha Iron & Nutrition Mission.

₹10L cashless per family30 medical colleges in 10 yearsDoctor ratio 1:1,735 → 1:1,000
12
ମାଟିରୁ ସମୃଦ୍ଧି
Matiru Samruddhi
Prosperity from the Soil

Legal guarantee of Minimum Support Price for paddy and all major crops. Payments credited within 7 days of procurement. ₹15,000/year per small/marginal farmer through the Krusi Income Protection Fund. One-time loan waiver for loans below ₹2 lakh. ₹3,000 crore Krusi Sangrakhyana Kosha for cold storage.

Legal MSP all major crops7-day payment₹15,000/year per farmer₹2L loan waiver₹3,000 crore cold storage
13
ଆଦିବାସୀ ଭୂମି
Adivasi Bhumi, Adivasira Hakk
Tribal Land, Tribal Rights

All pending Forest Rights Act claims settled within 12 months. Odisha Tribal Land Protection Act enacted. No forced displacement under any circumstance. Free, Prior, and Informed Consent from Gram Sabha mandatory for any project in scheduled areas. Minimum 10% community equity in resource profits on tribal lands.

FRA claims in 12 months10% community equity100 new Eklavya Schools35% kendu leaf bonus
14
ମହାନଦୀ ଆମ ଅଧିକାର
Mahanadi Ama Adhikar
Mahanadi is Our Right

Legally binding Mahanadi Tribunal award guaranteeing Odisha's equitable share. Statutory Mahanadi Water Rights Authority of Odisha established within the first year. Hirakud Reservoir irrigation safeguarded. Chilika Lake (Ramsar site) protected. 25% water-use efficiency improvement by 2030.

Water Authority year one2.5L hectares Hirakud irrigation2L fisherfolk on Chilika
15
ଖଣି ଓ ଖଣିଜ ସଂପଦ
Khanija Sampada, Janara Adhikar
Mineral Wealth, People's Right

At least 30% of Odisha's mineral revenue retained and reinvested within the state, prioritising value-added industries and local employment. 20% community equity in mining profits on tribal/local lands. Odisha Mining Restoration Authority. Odisha Downstream Manufacturing Mission. SEZ in Rourkela, Angul, Jharsuguda, Koraput.

43.7% of India's minerals from Odisha30% royalty ring-fenced20% community equity4 SEZ cities

12 Chapters. One Comprehensive Plan.

Party Profile

Founded 2026. Founding President Shri Mohammed Moquim. Six core ideology pillars. Registered office: Metro Office, A-295, Saheednagar, Bhubaneswar.

03
Odia Swabhimaan
ଓଡ଼ିଆ ସ୍ୱାଭିମାନ

Reclaiming Odisha's honour and rights. Why Odisha needs its own voice. The case for an Odia-first regional party.

04
15 Unbreakable Pledges

OJC's 15 non-negotiable pledges — named schemes, timelines, and public accountability.

05
Sakhyama Odisha — Vision 2036
ସଶକ୍ତ ଓଡ଼ିଶା

Eight outcomes by 2036, the centenary of Odisha statehood. Three phases of implementation.

06
Pragati Pathare Juba Odisha
ଯୁବ ଓଡ଼ିଶା

Education, Digital Odisha, Skill Development, Employment, Sports. Empowering youth, advancing Odisha.

Sasakta Naari, Samruddha Samaj

Nisa Mukta Odisha, Nari Sasaktikarana, Sasanare Naari ra Swara, Nirbhaya Naari — equal opportunities for women.
