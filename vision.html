OUR VISION

16 Unbreakable Pledges
for a Stronger Odisha

Our commitments for good governance, inclusive development, and a better future for every Odia.

01

Secular politics

Governance firmly rooted in the Constitution of India, ensuring equality before law and freedom from religious discrimination, division, or majoritarian politics.

Equal respect. Equal rights. One Odisha.
02

A youth-centric party

Prioritising education, skill development, employment, and entrepreneurship to ensure that Odisha's youth can build their future within the state.

Empowering youth. Inspiring leadership. Building tomorrow.
03

Women's empowerment

Commitment to full political, economic, and social equality for women, with guaranteed representation, safety, financial independence, and institutional support.

Empowered women. Stronger families. Progressive Odisha.
04

A party dedicated to Dalits, the oppressed, tribals and backward classes

Unwavering commitment to the dignity, constitutional rights, representation, and socio-economic advancement of Dalits, tribals, OBCs, and marginalized communities.

Social justice. Equal dignity. Inclusive growth.
05

Participation of society and of the people

Development driven by the needs and direct participation of citizens, where public policies are shaped by people's voices from every household.

Citizen led. Transparent power. Grassroots voice.
06

Priority to the development of farmers

Ensuring fair pricing, legal MSP guarantee, water security, income stability, and modern agricultural support for farmers as the backbone of Odisha.

Respect farmers. Reward hard work. Strengthen Odisha.
07

Emphasis on the rights of tribal people on natural resources

Uncompromising protection of tribal land, forest rights, cultural identity, and natural resources under Jal, Jungle, Jameen rights.

Protect land. Honor heritage. Tribal dignity.
08

Preservation, development and propagation of Odia pride, the distinct identity of the Odia people, and the Odia language, literature, culture and history

Promoting Odia language, literature, art, and historical heritage while protecting the self-respect and distinct identity of the Odia people nationwide.

Odia pride. Rich culture. Eternal identity.
09

Preservation of ideal democratic values, and moral support for the democratic process from the grassroots level upward

Upholding democratic ethics, free speech, and institutional integrity from Gram Panchayats upward to protect public trust in governance.

Grassroots democracy. Moral leadership. Public trust.
10

Protection of the constitutional rights of every section of society

Safeguarding individual liberty, justice, and fundamental constitutional rights for every citizen regardless of background, faith, or status.

Guaranteed rights. Legal safety. Constitutional rule.
11

Safeguarding the social, political and economic rights of citizens

Guaranteeing universal access to economic opportunity, political voice, and social security so that no household is left behind in progress.

Economic security. Social safety. Equal voice.
12

Transparency and accountability in the political and administrative system

Eradicating corruption through real-time governance dashboards, public audits, digital transparency, and strict accountability for public servants.

Zero corruption. Real accountability. Clean governance.
13

Better management of natural disasters and containment of the impact of damage

State-of-the-art early warning systems, resilient coastal infrastructure, rapid relief protocols, and long-term protection against cyclones and floods.

Rapid relief. Safe coastlines. Resilient Odisha.
14

Undertaking programmes for community-centred environmental protection and effective management of climate change

Citizen-led afforestation, river conservation including the Mahanadi, clean energy transition, and local environmental stewardship across districts.

Clean environment. Save rivers. Sustainable future.
15

Creating skill development, employment and entrepreneurial capability among young men and women from the school and college level onward

Integrating practical vocational training, tech skills, incubator funds, and entrepreneurship support directly into school and university curricula.

Industry skills. Local jobs. Young entrepreneurs.
16

Ensuring the conservation and judicious use of Odisha's natural resources, and making Odisha's future bright

Managing mineral wealth, water reservoirs, and natural assets responsibly for long-term public revenue, ecological balance, and generational prosperity.

Protect wealth. Judicious use. Bright future.

This vision belongs to you.
Hold us accountable.
